# Meal Strategies

Food, quantities, and swaps for closing a macro gap on a lean bulk. Macros below are
approximate — good enough to plan with, and `log_meal` does the real estimating.

---

## 1. Matching food to the gap

Read `remaining` from `get_today_macros()` and pick the row that matches the shape.

| Gap shape | What to reach for |
|---|---|
| **High protein, low carb, low fat** | Lean protein + volume. 200 g chicken breast, 250 g 0% Greek yoghurt, whey in water, 200 g white fish, egg whites |
| **High protein, high carb, low fat** | Chicken + rice, turkey mince + pasta, tuna + potatoes, yoghurt + honey + berries, whey + oats |
| **High carb, low protein** | Rice, potatoes, pasta, bagels, dried fruit, fruit juice, honey. Cheap and easy |
| **High fat only** | Nuts, olive oil on whatever is already cooked, nut butter, cheese, avocado, whole eggs |
| **Everything, large gap (>1,000 kcal)** | A full meal plus a shake. Don't try to close it with snacks |
| **Everything, small gap (<300 kcal)** | One snack. 40 g nuts, a shake with milk, yoghurt and fruit |
| **Calories done, protein short** | This is the common one. Whey in water, egg whites, 0% yoghurt, cottage cheese, protein "clear" drinks — protein without the calorie cost |
| **Protein done, calories short** | Add fat. Olive oil, nut butter, nuts. 1 tbsp olive oil = 120 kcal with zero effort |

## 3. Around the session

**Pre (1-2 h before), low fat and fibre:**

| Option | kcal | P | C | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 80 g oats + 30 g whey + banana | 570 | 38 | 85 | 9 |
| 2 bagels + 100 g turkey | 560 | 35 | 90 | 6 |
| 250 g white rice (cooked) + 150 g chicken | 500 | 40 | 60 | 6 |
| Whey shake + 2 rice cakes + honey | 320 | 27 | 45 | 3 |

**Post (within ~2 h):**

| Option | kcal | P | C | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 g whey + 60 g dextrose/maltodextrin | 400 | 33 | 62 | 3 |
| 300 g Greek yoghurt + 80 g granola + honey | 620 | 35 | 85 | 12 |
| Chicken + rice, straight into a real meal | 780 | 55 | 78 | 20 |
| 500 ml chocolate milk + 30 g whey | 440 | 42 | 47 | 10 |

**Pre-sleep:** 30-40 g casein, 250 g Greek yoghurt, or 200 g cottage cheese. Slow-digesting
protein overnight (Res et al. 2012). Easy place to bank the last protein feed of the day.

## 4. High-protein swaps

Same food, better macro shape. These are how a stubborn protein target gets hit without
blowing the calorie budget.

| Instead of | Use | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| Full-fat Greek yoghurt | 0% Greek yoghurt | +8 g P, −90 kcal per 250 g |
| Regular pasta | Lentil / chickpea pasta | +12 g P per 100 g dry |
| Standard bread | High-protein bread | +6 g P per 2 slices |
| Chicken thigh | Chicken breast | +6 g P, −80 kcal per 200 g |
| Regular milk | Skimmed or high-protein milk | +6 g P, −50 kcal per 500 ml |
| Regular cheese | Cottage cheese / light cheese | +10 g P, −120 kcal per 60 g |
| Standard mince (20%) | 5% lean mince | −140 kcal per 200 g |
| Standard ice cream | High-protein ice cream | +15 g P, −250 kcal per tub |
| Regular cereal | Cereal + 30 g whey stirred into the milk | +25 g P |
| Crisps | Beef jerky / biltong | +28 g P per 50 g |

Reverse them all when calories are the problem and protein is already met — full-fat versions,
oil on everything, whole milk. Getting calories in is the harder job on a big training day.

## 6. Practical patterns

**Prep once, eat four times.** Cook 1 kg of chicken/mince, 500 g dry rice, and a tray of
roast veg on Sunday. Four lunches assembled in two minutes each. The single highest-leverage
thing for hitting targets consistently — most missed targets are logistics failures, not
willpower failures.

**Anchor the protein.** Set four fixed protein anchors (breakfast, lunch, post-session, dinner)
and let carbs and fat flex around whatever the day brings. Protein is the macro that gets
missed; fixing it in place solves most of the problem.

**Liquid calories when appetite is the limiter.** A big training day at 3,500 kcal is a lot of
chewing. A shake with 60 g oats, 30 g whey, 300 ml whole milk, a banana and 20 g peanut butter
is ~800 kcal and takes 90 seconds to drink.

**Eating out.** Order the largest lean protein on the menu plus a carb side, ask for sauces on
the side, and don't try to hit the day exactly. One estimated meal inside a well-logged week is
noise. Log it as best you can and move on.

**Alcohol.** Blunts muscle protein synthesis for roughly 24 h at meaningful doses (Parr et al.
2014) and the calories are dead weight in a lean bulk. Not forbidden — just count it and keep
it away from training days.

**Fibre and micronutrients.** 30-40 g fibre a day, and two portions of vegetables at lunch and
dinner. A surplus built on white rice and whey works on paper and feels terrible by week six.

**When appetite crashes** (common in weeks 3-4 of a mesocycle): shift toward calorie-dense,
low-volume foods — oils, nut butters, dried fruit, whole milk, white rice over brown. Drop
fibre slightly. It usually resolves after the deload.
