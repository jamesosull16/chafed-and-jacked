# Exercise Library

Full commercial gym: rack, barbells, dumbbells, cables, and full machine selection including
leg press, hack squat, hamstring curl (lying and seated), and hip thrust machine.

## How to read the columns

- **Chain** — `post` posterior, `ant` anterior, `neu` neutral/both.
- **Pattern** — movement pattern for balance accounting.
- **Ham-len** — hamstring **lengthened-load risk**: how much the movement loads the proximal
  hamstring in a lengthened, hip-flexed position. **low** = safe from week 1. **mod** = stage 2-3,
  introduce with controlled range. **high** = stage 4 only, late block, after symptom-free
  full-range loading.
- **Knee** — knee flexion demand: low / mod / high. High = deep flexion under load; manage volume.
- **DF** — ankle dorsiflexion demand: low / mod / high. High = restricted ankles will limit it;
  elevate heels or substitute.

Early block, default to **Ham-len low** and **Knee ≤ mod**. Substitution table is in
`injury-guardrails.md`.

## Calves — priority 3, respond to frequency

| Exercise | Chain | Pattern | Ham-len | Knee | DF | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standing calf raise | post | plantarflexion | low | low | low | Gastroc (knee straight). Full range with a pause at the bottom — doubles as DF mobility |
| Smith-machine calf raise | post | plantarflexion | low | low | low | Easiest to load heavy and progress |
| Seated calf raise | post | plantarflexion | low | mod | low | Soleus (knee bent). Needs its own slot — the standing version doesn't cover it |
| Leg-press calf press | post | plantarflexion | low | low | low | Convenient superset with leg press |
| Single-leg calf raise | post | plantarflexion | low | low | low | Left/right corrective |
| Weighted DF hold (loaded stretch) | post | mobility | low | low | low | Bottom-position hold. Mobility and hypertrophy at once |

Hit calves 2-3×/week. Pause 1-2s at the bottom of every rep; the loaded stretch is where both
the growth and the dorsiflexion range come from.

**Reading the risk columns.** `Ham. stretch` is loaded lengthening of the
*proximal* hamstring: `high` is blocked until block week 13, `moderate` until
week 5, `low` is available from week 1. `Knee flexion` `high` is blocked
outright while the knee flag is set; `moderate` is permitted with a capped ROM
and a 3-second eccentric. `Ankle DF` never blocks — anything above `low` gets
heel elevation.
